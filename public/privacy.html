Executive Assistant Privacy Policy

Updated October 26, 2026

Executive Assistant is a personal productivity workspace operated by Mike Wissa. This policy describes how the hosted app and its ChatGPT/Codex plugin handle information, including the optional WHOOP connection. Optional features operate only when configured and explicitly authorized; publishing this policy does not connect an account or grant an assistant access.

Information handled

After you sign in and approve access, the service may process the schedule, tasks, notes, profile preferences, briefings, and audit history stored in your Executive Assistant account. OAuth credentials are used to identify your account and authorize each request.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide workspace features you request, such as reading current context, searching notes, updating tasks or focus plans, and saving briefings. Agent-authored changes are recorded in the app's audit history.

Storage and sharing

The website is hosted on Vercel, and account and workspace data are stored with Supabase. These providers process information needed to operate the service, including routine request and security logs. When you use the plugin, relevant data is returned to the ChatGPT or Codex client acting on your behalf. Notebook content you explicitly share is available to the people you authorize. The app does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Optional WHOOP connection

If you choose to connect WHOOP, you authorize access on WHOOP's own sign-in and consent screens. Executive Assistant does not receive your WHOOP password. It requests read access to sleep, recovery, and physiological cycles, plus offline authorization so the connection can refresh in the background.

The app reads WHOOP records and retains only a limited private summary: recovery score and scoring status, sleep duration and performance, cycle strain, cycle identifiers, relevant dates/timezone offsets, and sync status. It does not retain raw sensor recordings or complete API responses, and does not request workout, body-measurement, or basic-profile permissions. Access and refresh credentials are encrypted server-side and are not provided to assistants.

These summaries support your personal daily planning and are not medical advice, diagnosis, or a measure of your ability to work. The app does not automatically change tasks or meetings based on a score. WHOOP data is not automatically copied into shared notebooks, work briefings, tasks, email, or the app's general memory index.

WHOOP sharing and assistant consent

Connecting WHOOP does not by itself authorize ChatGPT, Codex, or another assistant to read your health summary. You must separately select each approved assistant connection in Profile. Existing workspace permission alone does not grant WHOOP access. You can keep the summary app-only or remove an assistant's WHOOP access without disconnecting WHOOP.

When you expressly grant access, that assistant and its provider receive the private summary to answer your requests and support your personal planning, under their own terms, privacy policies, and account settings. Executive Assistant does not use WHOOP data to train or improve AI models. Revocation cannot recall information already returned to an assistant; manage that provider's conversation history and data controls separately.

WHOOP retention and deletion

Cached cycle summaries have a 30-day retention limit. Minimal private connection/sync audit events, which do not contain health scores or credentials, have a 90-day retention limit. Routine maintenance removes expired records. Connection metadata and encrypted credentials are kept while needed for the authorized connection.

When the WHOOP feature is enabled, Profile provides controls to view the summary, remove assistant access, and disconnect. Disconnect immediately removes local assistant access, saved credentials, pending authorization state, and cached readings from the active app database. Minimal audit and disconnected-status records may remain within the limits above. It also attempts to revoke the WHOOP grant; if revocation cannot be confirmed, remove the grant in WHOOP as well. Disconnect does not delete your original WHOOP records.

You can request access to or deletion of app-held information using the contact address below. Copies in hosting or database backups and service logs may remain until those providers' normal retention periods expire; this policy does not promise immediate removal from those systems.

Workspace controls

You can revoke an approved agent from Profile → Agent connections. You can also edit or delete workspace content through the app. Revocation prevents that OAuth client from making future requests.

Security

Agent requests require a valid Supabase OAuth token and a separate active approval for the same client. WHOOP summaries also require the additional per-assistant authorization described above. Access is scoped to the signed-in user's records, and connections use HTTPS. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes and third-party services

Material changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date. WHOOP and any assistant you authorize remain separate services governed by their own policies. Executive Assistant is an independent personal app, not a WHOOP-operated or endorsed service.
