Executive Assistant Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

Executive Assistant is a personal productivity workspace. This policy describes how the hosted app and its ChatGPT/Codex plugin handle information.

Information handled

After you sign in and approve access, the service may process the schedule, tasks, notes, profile preferences, briefings, and audit history stored in your Executive Assistant account. OAuth credentials are used to identify your account and authorize each request.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide workspace features you request, such as reading current context, searching notes, updating tasks or focus plans, and saving briefings. Agent-authored changes are recorded in the app's audit history.

Storage and sharing

Workspace data is stored with Supabase. When you use the plugin, relevant data is returned to the ChatGPT or Codex client acting on your behalf. The app does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Your controls

You can revoke an approved agent from Profile → Agent connections. You can also edit or delete workspace content through the app. Revocation prevents that OAuth client from making future requests.

Security

The integration requires a valid Supabase OAuth token and a separate active approval for the same client. Access is scoped to the signed-in user's records.
